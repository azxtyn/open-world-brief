How it works

  1. Official sources only. Every video starts from an official page: Rockstar Games, PlayStation, Xbox or Take-Two. No rumours, no leaks, no guesses.
  2. Word for word. Each fact is a sentence copied from the official page, and the engine checks it against that exact page before a script is written.
  3. Nothing that goes stale. Sentences like “today” or “two weeks away”, sales talk and opinions are left out, so a video is still true when you watch it.
  4. Clearly labeled. The voice is AI and every description says so, lists the sources and says this is an unofficial fan channel.
